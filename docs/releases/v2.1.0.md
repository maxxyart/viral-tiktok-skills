# Viral Camp social analysis skills v2.1.0

**Social Account Hook Analysis** gets a new implementation. Social Account Short Analysis is unchanged.

## What the hook skill does now

- **Sample.** With no count the whole account is analyzed when it has ≤150 posts, else the latest 50 with a warning that older hits are outside the sample. `--count`, `--all` and `--since YYYY-MM-DD` override it; pinned posts are handled for `--since`. On a test account of 87 posts the old fixed 50 would have dropped 8 of its 13 hits.
- **Instagram carousels and photos.** `--ig-source posts` reads carousels, photos and reels from one feed. Instagram has no views for carousels and photos, so these runs compare likes and say so in the report.
- **Frame 0 for videos.** The cover of a video is the first frame the feed shows, not the grid thumbnail the creator picked.
- **Young posts.** Posts younger than 7 days stay out of every median and split but are still listed as hits.
- **Themes and product fit.** `labels.json` per post (theme, `direct` / `adjacent` / `off`) becomes splits in the stats and the CSV.
- **Verbatim slide texts.** `carousels/NN/slides.json` stores what the agent read on the slides of the hits.
- **ER** = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / views × 100, the same formula as the short analysis.
- **Small groups.** Every pattern and split bucket under 3 posts is flagged and drawn as a thin bar.
- **Report.** Hero sample note, virals slide by slide, product adaptations tied to source posts, patterns, splits by format / month / theme / fit, and a grid of all posts sortable by date or views. It opens locally or publishes as a claude.ai Artifact in batches.
- **Layout check.** `snapshot_report.py` loads the page in headless Chrome at 390 px (inside an iframe, since Chrome will not open a window under 500 px) and at 1280 px, fails on horizontal overflow or broken images, and screenshots every section.
- **CSV and archive.** `export_csv.py` writes one row per post and `--save-to` copies the whole run into your project.

## Upgrade

Replace the installed `social-account-hook-analysis` folder. The v2.0.0 implementation (contact sheets of 20, audio transcription, self-contained HTML) is frozen in `legacy/skills/social-account-hook-analysis-v2/` with `INSTRUCTIONS.md` instead of a discoverable `SKILL.md`; its helpers also stay in `src/social/`.

Requirements: Python 3.10+, Pillow, `ffmpeg`, Google Chrome / Chromium for the layout check, `SCRAPE_CREATORS_API_KEY` in the environment or an explicit `--env-file` (no automatic search of shell configs). FAST mode only: `GOOGLE_API_KEY`, `XAI_API_KEY` as fallback.

## Validation

7 new offline tests (`tests/test_hook_analysis.py`) cover young-post exclusion, small-group and month flags, the ER formula, likes mode, strict cluster validation, the report sections and the CSV export. The v2.0.0 tests now run against the legacy copy. Live runs: a TikTok carousel account with 87 posts (fetch 64 s, layout check 8 s, no overflow at 390 px) and 12 Instagram posts in `--ig-source posts` mode.
